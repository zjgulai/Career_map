# Buyer prospect shortlist

For a small shortlist, prefer direct public-source research and a concise table rather than serial research delegations plus a separate report-writing phase. If delegation materially helps, use non-overlapping assignments and consolidate returned evidence promptly; do not wait to start each independent group until the previous group finishes. Deliver verified rows and the remaining gap if research stalls instead of leaving only an opening progress message.

Read prior product research and catalogs. Identify what the user sells and who buys it. Packaging factories selling to brands or fillers are not automatically Amazon retail sellers.
If the user gives materials, production capabilities and target buyer roles, begin prospect research without demanding a retail ASIN or landed-cost model.

Discover available public business research tools. For each named company provide:
- legal/trading name and official domain;
- country and buyer role (brand, importer, distributor, filler, retailer);
- concrete product/category overlap with source URL and observed date;
- purchasing fit and why the user's manufacturing capability matters;
- public business contact or vendor-onboarding page; unknown if inaccessible;
- target business role and individualized first-message draft;
- one qualification question and next step.

Separate observed category fit from inferred buying interest. Never promise likely conversion or describe public prospects as confirmed buyers. Rank by explicit fit/accessible channel, not invented success probability.
For "20 customers", provide up to 20 distinct evidenced accounts, state actual verified count and remaining gaps. A search strategy, blank CRM template or supplier list is not a customer list.
Keep upstream suppliers and downstream buyers in separate columns. Preserve all applicable product restrictions. Drafts stay unsent. For "continue" after a correction, produce missing account-level rows before expanding strategy prose.
If public company research is unavailable or returns no supported prospects, state the actual evidence gap and verified count. Do not invent companies or convert the task into an Amazon retail approval. Ask for a target-account list, official company URLs with accessible page content, or an existing public prospect export, then explain how product/category fit and per-account approach can be assessed. Missing purchase intent is different from missing company evidence: companies with verified category fit may remain labelled prospects even when purchasing plans or contacts are unknown. Deliver verified accounts now and mark unknown fields; do not discard usable prospects solely because actual buying interest is unproven, and do not promise that supplying a catalog alone reveals real buyers.

An input request is not the only next step: if the task remains incomplete, offer multiple feasible choices, such as Agent-led research into a narrower buyer segment or analysis of existing evidenced prospects first; put optional user target lists/exports last. Name and link the actual company evidence beside the matching claim, separating observed fit from inferred buying interest. Apply the shared response contract without another Skill load.
