# Accounting and Tax Compliance Knowledge Engine

## Capabilities

Provide the Tax Compliance Expert team with a core reference knowledge base, including:
- A standard chart of accounts under China's Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises (first- through third-level accounts, codes, and descriptions)
- Quick-reference tables for tax rates (VAT/corporate income tax/individual income tax/surcharges/stamp duty/property tax, etc.)
- Common journal entry templates (purchases/sales/expenses/payroll/VAT transfers/profit and loss closing entries)

## References

Read the following references before use for detailed information:
- @references/chart-of-accounts.md — Standard chart of accounts under China's Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises (first through third levels) and common journal entries
- @references/tax-rates.md — Quick-reference guide to Chinese tax rates (subject to the latest policies)

## Usage

Team members may consult these references when performing tasks to ensure that:
- Account codes and names comply with the standards
- Tax rates are applied correctly
- Journal entries use the correct debit and credit directions
- Financial statement formats comply with the requirements

## Output Format

Reference data is presented in structured Markdown tables for quick lookup.

## Notes

- Use the latest chart of accounts published by China's Ministry of Finance as the authoritative version
- Follow the latest policies if tax rates change
- Confirm local tax incentives based on the company's place of registration
